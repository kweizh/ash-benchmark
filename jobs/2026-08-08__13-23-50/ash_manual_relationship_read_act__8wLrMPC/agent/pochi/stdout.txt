6. **`fork` create action** on `Thread`, implemented by `ThreadGraph.Forum.Threads.ForkThread` (manual create). Validates the source message/thread up front (returning `Ash.Error.Changes.InvalidArgument` with `field: :source_message_id` and the exact required messages, with nothing persisted on failure), then copies the source and all transitive descendants into a new thread with renumbered positions and reproduced parent/child structure, plus one `fork_of` `MessageLink`.

7. **Domain code interfaces** on `ThreadGraph.Forum`: `highlights/2,3,4` + `highlights!/2,3,4` (→ `cross_board_highlights`, args `[:boards, :min_endorsements]`) and `fork_thread/3,4,5` + `fork_thread!/3,4,5` (→ `fork`, args `[:source_message_id, :slug, :title]`).

Each of the five new modules bumps its designated `LoadCounter` key exactly once per invocation.

Verified via `mix compile --warnings-as-errors` (clean) and ad-hoc `mix run` scripts exercising: the aggregate, all three relationships (including nested loads of `reply_count`/`ancestor_messages` underneath `recent_messages`), read-count telemetry for K=15 source records (1, 1, and 2 reads respectively — well under the 8-read cap), `cross_board_highlights` filtering/sorting/metadata, and the `fork` action's happy path plus both failure modes (confirmed zero records created on failure via before/after counts). All scratch/introspection scripts were removed after verification.
